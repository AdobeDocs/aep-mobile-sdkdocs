# Live Activities and Live Updates

Live Activities (iOS) and Live Updates (Android) display real-time, glanceable information from your app - such as the progress of an order, a ride, or a live sports score - outside of the app itself. With the Adobe Journey Optimizer Messaging extension, you can remotely start, update, and end them through push notifications.

<InlineAlert variant="info" slots="text"/>

**Live Activities** is an iOS feature built on [ActivityKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities) (Lock Screen and Dynamic Island). **Live Updates** is the Android counterpart, built on Android 16 (API 36) promoted ongoing notifications ("live" status-bar chips). Both are driven by the same Adobe Journey Optimizer campaigns; the client integration differs per platform.
